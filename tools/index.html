How to use these tools

Start with the Home Energy Audit if you don't know where your home stands — fifteen questions, an estimated EPC band, and your top three recommendations. Then work the quick wins: the LED calculator, standby checker, draught-proofing and cylinder jacket calculators all pay back in under two years, most in months.

When you're ready to plan properly, the Whole-House Retrofit Planner sequences everything into three phases — quick wins, fabric, then systems — inside your budget. The EPC Improvement Estimator shows what each measure does to your rating. And before you spend serious money, check the Grant & Subsidy Finder: thousands of pounds of support go unclaimed every year.

One house rule runs through every tool here: the maths is honest, including when it says a measure doesn't pay. Heat pumps can cost more to run than gas at current price ratios. Batteries rarely pay for themselves. Double glazing pays back in decades. We'd rather you know that before the quote than after the invoice.
